# Synthetic Access Standard

<!-- eval-chunk:c01 -->
## Identity
Every fictional Northstar operator must use an individually assigned account. Shared operator accounts are prohibited. Multi-factor authentication is required for every privileged account.

<!-- eval-chunk:c02 -->
## Access review
Team leads review privileged access every 30 days. A review record lists the account, its owner, the reviewer, and the decision.

<!-- eval-chunk:c03 -->
## Credential handling
Access tokens expire after 12 hours. Tokens must not be copied into support tickets or chat messages. A suspected exposed token is revoked immediately and reported to the security response queue.

<!-- eval-chunk:c04 -->
## Access removal
Unused privileged access is removed within one business day after it is identified.
