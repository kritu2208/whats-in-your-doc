# Synthetic Support Playbook

<!-- eval-chunk:c01 -->
## Ticket acknowledgement
The fictional service desk acknowledges routine tickets within four business hours. An acknowledgement includes a ticket reference and the next expected update time; it does not promise that the issue will already be resolved.

<!-- eval-chunk:c02 -->
## Priority targets
Priority P1 tickets receive an initial response within 15 minutes, at any hour. Priority P2 tickets receive an initial response within two staffed service-desk hours. These are response targets, not resolution guarantees.

<!-- eval-chunk:c03 -->
## Closure
A ticket can be closed after the requester confirms resolution or after two unanswered follow-ups sent on separate business days. The agent records the closure reason and the final action taken.
