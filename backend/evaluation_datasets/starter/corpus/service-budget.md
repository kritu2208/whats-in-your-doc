# Synthetic Service Budget

<!-- eval-chunk:c01 -->
## Monthly cap
The fictional Northstar sandbox has a monthly infrastructure cap of 240 credits. The cap resets at 00:00 UTC on the first day of each calendar month. Unused credits do not roll over.

<!-- eval-chunk:c02 -->
## Alerts
Budget alerts are sent to the service owner at 70 percent and 90 percent of the monthly cap. Crossing an alert threshold does not automatically stop the sandbox.

<!-- eval-chunk:c03 -->
## Exceptions
Spending above the monthly cap requires written approval from both the service owner and finance partner before the additional spending occurs. The approval reference is recorded with the monthly cost report.
